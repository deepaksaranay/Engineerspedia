Mechanical Engineering Toolkit

Four quick calculators for everyday mechanical work — stress & strain, beam deflection, gear ratio & torque, and power/torque/RPM. Enter your values; results update instantly, nothing leaves your browser.

Stress & Strain

Axial stress from force and cross-sectional area — plus strain and Young's modulus if you supply the elongation.

—

Formulas used
σ = F / A ε = ΔL / L₀ E = σ / ε (Young's modulus, if ΔL and L₀ given)

Beam Deflection

Maximum deflection and bending stress for a simply supported beam with a central point load.

—

Formulas used
δ_max = P·L³ / (48·E·I) (simply supported, central point load) M_max = P·L / 4 σ = M_max·c / I

Gear Ratio & Torque

Output speed and torque for a simple two-gear (or belt/chain) drive, from tooth counts.

—

Formulas used
Gear ratio (GR) = N₂ / N₁ (driven / driver) Output RPM = Input RPM / GR Output torque = Input torque × GR (ideal, 100% efficiency)

Power · Torque · RPM

Solve for whichever value you're missing — provide any two of Power, Torque and RPM.

—

Formulas used
P (W) = T (N·m) × ω (rad/s) ω = RPM × 2π / 60 ⇒ P = T × RPM × 2π / 60
Worth knowing: these are ideal, textbook formulas (no safety factors, no efficiency losses). Use them to sanity-check a design or homework, not as a substitute for proper engineering analysis on anything load-bearing.
